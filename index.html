Physical VS Digital

Yo He-Yo is a game about the revoltion of a fully digital world enforced by GovTech. The Playrez are the only rebel group who are willing to go against the government's strict digital policy on the world.

Fight against the opposing digital forces with your own Valued Possessions and take back Sunscorer City!
